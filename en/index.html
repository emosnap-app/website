FAQ

A few questions before you start.

Where is my data stored?

All data stays on your device. Nothing is sent to servers or stored in the cloud. Your privacy is protected.

What's free?

Emotion recording and calendar review are free. With Emo+ (monthly subscription), you can track mood trends over time, get AI-powered morning reflections based on yesterday's record, and receive a weekly summary every Monday. 30-day free trial available.

Who is this for?

People who find writing exhausting, those who struggle to express feelings openly, or anyone who takes photos but has no record of how they actually felt.

Should I start now?

If the cause of your stress feels blurry, now is the right time. Try it for a week. If it doesn't fit, just stop. That's all.
