EmoSnap Privacy Policy (App)

Last updated: 2026-05-20

1. Basic Policy

EmoSnap (the "App") respects user privacy and limits the information it collects to what is necessary to provide the service. This policy explains how the App handles personal information.

2. Information Collected

  • User-provided content: Photos, emoji, and text notes saved within the App. This content is managed on your device.
  • Device information: OS version, device model, language settings, crash logs (for service improvement and bug analysis).
  • Analytics data: Anonymized usage statistics (when analytics are enabled).
  • Purchase information: Transactions via the App Store are managed by Apple. The App only retains purchase status information. Payment details (card numbers, etc.) are never held by the App.

3. How We Use Information

  • Providing core app functionality (saving, displaying, and managing photos and notes)
  • Analytics for service improvement, UX enhancement, and bug fixes
  • Preventing unauthorized use and maintaining security

4. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party SDKs and services:

  • App analytics: Firebase Analytics (Google)
  • Device info: DeviceKit, etc.
  • Image editing: CropViewController, etc.

These services have their own privacy policies, and data handling is governed by their respective terms. Data is shared with third parties only as required by law or with user consent, and only to the minimum extent necessary.

5. Data Storage and Deletion

Photos and notes saved in the App are stored on your device. If server-side storage is introduced, it will be clearly disclosed along with the retention period and deletion method. Data can be deleted through in-app deletion operations.

6. Your Rights

You may request disclosure, correction, or deletion of your information held by the App. Please contact us using the details at the end of this policy.

7. Security

The App implements appropriate technical and organizational measures to prevent data leaks, tampering, and unauthorized access. However, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

8. Minors

If used by a minor, parental consent is required. Personal information of minors is handled with particular care.

9. International Data Transfers

Data may be processed internationally through third-party services. In such cases, appropriate protections are applied in accordance with applicable laws.
